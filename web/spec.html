1Purpose

Zenatech evaluates more than 200 candidate events a year across its business units (DaaS, ZD, IQ Nano, ZT and SaaS). Before Compass, that pipeline lived in spreadsheets and email threads. Nobody had one view of who was going where, what it cost, what had been shipped, or what came back from it.

Compass replaces that with a single, live, invite-only web app. It covers the whole life of an event:

  • Choose: find and score candidate events, including AI-assisted discovery.
  • Plan: budget, approval, team, collateral and travel.
  • Run: readiness tracking, milestones, alerts and reminders.
  • Learn: post-event reports with photos, cost per lead and an impact score that feed next year's choices.

2What it stands for

Every show, every traveler, one map.

The name is the idea. A compass gives direction. It doesn't make the trip for you; it makes sure you're heading the right way. Compass does the same for Zenatech's event program, in four ways:

Direction

Spend event budget where it pays back. Every event carries an objective, a priority, a cost per lead and an impact score.

One source of truth

One list of events, people and materials that everyone sees live, instead of copies in inboxes.

Accountability

Every change is logged, budgets need approval, and every attendee closes the loop with a report.

Global by design

A world map at the center, because Zenatech's events, people and stock are spread across countries and time zones.

Its principles are simple. The app is invite-only and secure by default, gives people the least access their role needs, is quick to use on a phone at a booth, and emails people only when something concerns them.

5What it does

Sign-in and accounts

  • Sign-in: email and password, with a minimum of 10 characters.
  • Invites: an Admin chooses an email and a role. The invite link is emailed, lasts 7 days and can also be copied.
  • Account creation: only invited emails can create an account, and the email address must be confirmed.
  • Password resets: self-service by email, or sent by an Admin.
  • Account settings: change password, sign out, and an Email alerts on/off switch.
  • Turning an account off: blocks all data access immediately.

Mission Control (dashboard)

  • Key figures: tracked events, events in the next 90 days, events at risk (under 70% ready within 30 days), committed spend, people traveling and average readiness.
  • World map: flat map or rotating globe, with every event pinned and colored by status.
  • Lists: "Up next" and "Needs attention".
  • Alerts: travel not booked within 14 days of an event, and people double-booked on overlapping events.

Events pipeline

  • Table: filters by year, status, business unit and text search.
  • Statuses: Investigating Confirmed Waitlist / Next Year Completed Will Not Attend Again
  • Each event has seven tabs: Overview, Cost & Impact, Team, Collateral, Travel, Progress and Feedback.
  • Readiness score (0–100): cost entered 20, team assigned 25, required collateral packed 25, status confirmed 15, website on file 15.
  • Budget gate: an event can't become Confirmed or Completed until an Admin approves its budget.

Project Map

  • Milestones: Paid, Staffed and Resourced (set manually), and Travel Planned, Checked In and Attended (set automatically from travelers' own updates).

My Conferences

  • Attendee home page: my events, my travel status, and quick buttons to update travel or write a report.

Resource Map and Inventory

  • Resource Map: shows where people and materials are going, with routes from each traveler's home city.
  • Assigning: drag (or tap) a person or item onto an event.
  • Inventory: stock by location, with over-allocation blocked.

Team & Roster, Collateral Library

  • Roster: people with title, email and home location.
  • CSV import: includes a preview, duplicate detection and validation.
  • Collateral Library: items with stock per location and a "required for every event" flag.

Budget and Analytics

  • Budget: spend by business unit, quarter and year, with CSV export.
  • Analytics: total spend, expected leads, blended cost per lead, average impact, and best and worst events by cost per lead.

Feedback (post-event reports)

  • Content: purpose, summary, recommendation, follow-ups, links and photos.
  • Visibility: attendees see their own reports; Managers and Admins see all of them.
  • Word export: one click produces a formatted .docx with the photos embedded.

Discover (AI event research)

  • Search: live web search by industry, region and timeframe, using Claude with web search.
  • Results: name, dates, venue, city, expected audience, why it fits, the official website and sources.
  • Duplicates: events already in the pipeline are flagged.
  • Add to pipeline: one click creates an Investigating event.

Activity Log and live updates

  • Activity Log: who did what and when, keeping the newest 1,000 entries.
  • Live updates: changes appear for everyone without reloading.
  • Concurrent edits: two people editing the same event don't overwrite each other.
  • Devices and themes: works on desktop, tablet and phone, in light and dark mode.

8Security

  • Row-level security on every table: nothing is readable without signing in, and turned-off accounts see nothing.
  • Rules are enforced in the database, not just the screen: attendees can only change their own team membership and travel, and only Admins change roles.
  • Invite enforcement: the database refuses sign-ups from emails that weren't invited.
  • Secrets: the AI key, the email password and the notification secret live only in Supabase secrets and Vault, never in code. The key in config.js is public by design.
  • Tested: 24 permission tests pass against the live project.

9Quality targets

  • Scale: about 50 users and thousands of events. The data loads once, then updates live.
  • Availability: runs on the free tiers of GitHub Pages and Supabase. Supabase Pro is recommended for daily backups and no auto-pause.
  • Privacy: work data only (names, titles, work email, home city, travel status). Photos are stored under unguessable names.
  • Accessibility: keyboard-usable controls, visible focus, labelled fields, and tap alternatives to drag-and-drop.

10Out of scope (this version)

  • Booking and payment: Compass doesn't book travel or pay invoices; it tracks their status.
  • Leads and CRM: there's no lead capture or CRM sync; expected leads are entered by hand.
  • Single sign-on: Microsoft or Google sign-in isn't included, but can be added later.
  • Native mobile app: not planned; the web app works on phones.
